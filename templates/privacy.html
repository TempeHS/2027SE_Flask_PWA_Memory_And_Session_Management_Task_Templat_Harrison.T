Privacy Policy

This app stores the minimum data needed to take your canteen order.

  • Cookie session_id: a random ID that links your browser to your session on the server. It is HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax and expires after 30 minutes.
  • Cookie session: a signed cookie that holds the CSRF token protecting the forms.
  • Server session: your first name and the items in your current order. It is deleted after 2 minutes of inactivity or when you sign out.
  • Database: placed orders (your first name, the items and the total) so the canteen can prepare them.
  • localStorage key myOrder: a copy of your order summary for when you are offline.
